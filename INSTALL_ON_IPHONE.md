# Install FDR Kart Setup Data on iPhone (PWA)

**Fastest way to get the app on your iPhone without waiting for TestFlight approval.**

## Option 1: Add to Home Screen (Recommended)

This installs the app as a standalone app icon on your iPhone home screen. Works offline and updates automatically.

### Steps:

1. **Open Safari** on your iPhone
2. **Go to:** `https://kartsetup-fftu9jqd.manus.space`
3. **Wait for the app to load** (you'll see the Kart Setup logo)
4. **Tap the Share button** (box with arrow at bottom)
5. **Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"**
6. **Name:** Keep as "Kart Setup" or customize
7. **Tap "Add"** in the top right
8. **Done!** The app icon now appears on your home screen

### Launch:
- Tap the "Kart Setup" icon on your home screen
- App opens in full-screen mode (no Safari address bar)
- Works offline with cached data

## Option 2: Bookmark (Quick Access)

If you prefer not to add an icon to your home screen:

1. Open Safari
2. Go to `https://kartsetup-fftu9jqd.manus.space`
3. Tap Share → Add Bookmark
4. Save to your favorites or home screen

## Troubleshooting

### "Add to Home Screen" option not showing?
- Make sure you're using **Safari** (not Chrome or other browsers)
- Try closing Safari completely and reopening
- Ensure you're on iOS 15+

### App not loading?
- Check your internet connection
- Clear Safari cache: Settings → Safari → Clear History and Website Data
- Try again in a few minutes

### Need to update the app?
- The app checks for updates automatically
- You'll see a notification when an update is available
- Tap to install the latest version
